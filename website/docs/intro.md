# Welcome to Stock Analyst

**Stock Analyst** is an add-on, called a *skill*, for AI assistants such as [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code). Once installed, you can ask your assistant about stocks in plain English:

> "Give me a quick score on Apple."
>
> "Write a full report on Reliance Industries."
>
> "Compare Microsoft and Google."
>
> "How did our past calls do?"

The assistant then fetches real market data, scores the stock, does research and explains it in plain words.

## What makes it different

Most stock tools forget everything when you close them. This one keeps a **journal** of every call it makes. About a month later it checks how each call turned out, works out what it got wrong, and writes that down as a **lesson**. It reads those lessons, plus what it knows about how *you* invest, before every new analysis. The more you use it, the more it fits you.

## What it can do

| You say | It does |
|---|---|
| "Quick score on NVDA" | Gives a score from -100 to +100 in seconds, with the reasons behind it |
| "Full report on TCS.NS" | Writes a researched report with bull case, bear case, valuation and risks |
| "Compare AAPL vs MSFT" | Ranks the stocks side by side |
| "I bought 10 AAPL at 180" | Adds them to your portfolio tracker |
| "Show my portfolio" | Shows your profit and loss and flags risks |
| "Review our past calls" | Checks old calls against what really happened and learns from them |

## How long does setup take?

About **15-20 minutes** the first time, most of it waiting for downloads. You don't need to know how to code. Every step tells you exactly what to type.

:::caution Not financial advice
This is a research tool. It can be wrong, and its data can be late or incomplete. Always do your own research and never invest money you can't afford to lose.
:::
