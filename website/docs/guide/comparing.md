# Comparing stocks

Can't decide between two or more stocks? Ask Claude to compare them.

## How to ask

```text
Compare AAPL vs MSFT
Which is better: TCS.NS or INFY.NS?
Rank NVDA, AMD and INTC
```

## What you get

- A **quick score for each**, with a ranking
- **Side-by-side tables** for growth, profitability, valuation and debt
- **Where each one wins**: "Microsoft has higher margins; Apple is growing faster"
- A **recommendation**: which looks more attractive and why, sometimes with a suggested split

:::tip
Compare companies in the **same industry** (two banks, two chip makers). Comparing a bank with a tech company is like comparing apples with bicycles: the numbers mean different things.
:::
