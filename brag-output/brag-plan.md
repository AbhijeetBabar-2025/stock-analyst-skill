# Brag plan: Stock Analyst

**What it is:** A free skill for Claude Code that scores any stock or ETF, explains the why, tracks your portfolio, and grades its own past calls.
**For:** Retail investors (US and India) who want a second opinion with reasons, not a black-box rating.
**Sets it apart:** It logs every call and checks itself against the market after 30 days.
**Visual hook:** The real dashboard, typing tickers and lighting up score cards.
**Tone:** `default` leaning polished. Clean, confident, dark fintech UI. No hype words.
**Share caption:** Type a ticker, get a score, a bull and bear case, and a skill that grades its own calls.

## Angle

Show the actual dashboard doing its job, start to finish, in 20 seconds. Every UI frame is the real app (its own HTML, CSS and JS) fed with real scan data captured on 23 Oct 2026.

## Storyboard (21.5s, 1920x1080, 30fps)

| Time | Scene | On screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-3.2 | Hook | Tickers typed into the scanner: NVDA, AAPL, GLD, RELIANCE.NS | Type a ticker. / Get a score in seconds. |

| 15.6-18.0 | Journal | Journal tab with logged calls, "too early" until 30 days | Every call logged. Then checked. |
| 18.0-21.5 | Outro | Logo, name, install command, docs URL | A free skill for Claude Code that learns from every call. |

## Sound

Original synth track, A minor to C major, 96 BPM: pad from the start, kick and bass enter on the scan click, pluck arpeggio under the analysis, resolving C major add9 chord on the outro. SFX in key and mixed under the music: typing ticks, E6 click ticks, filtered whooshes for the panel and outro, pentatonic chimes as cards land, C major sparkle on the logo.

## Data and stand-ins

- Scores, prices, the GLD analysis panel and headlines are real output from the skill on 23 Oct 2026.
- **The portfolio is fictional sample holdings** (12 AAPL, 20 NVDA, 6 GLD, 40 RELIANCE.NS, 15 TCS.NS). Prices and scores on it are real.
- Journal shows calls logged from the dashboard that day; outcomes read "too early", as they would.
- No personal data, keys or private URLs appear (127.0.0.1 is the local dashboard address).
